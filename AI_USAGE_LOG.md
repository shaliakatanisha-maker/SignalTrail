## Things the group must be able to explain in the interview
* Why latency includes connection setup (`Connection: close` → new TCP + TLS per probe), and why the
  watchdog enforces a 15 s total budget (`HttpProber.kt`).
* The API-level branches: `WifiManager.getConnectionInfo()` (≤30) vs `NetworkCapabilities.transportInfo`
  (31+); `PhoneStateListener` (≤30) vs `TelephonyCallback` (31+); `getCellSignalStrengths()` dBm (29+);
  `getDataNetworkType()` permission (`SignalReader.kt`).
* How a mismatch is handled (request not sent, row saved) vs `connection_changed` (excluded in analysis).
* Why the session, not the request, is the unit of repetition in `analyze.py`.
